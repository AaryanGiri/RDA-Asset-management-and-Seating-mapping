Locus · Workplace OS — how it works

Seating & Asset Management, end to end

A one-page map of the two everyday flows: managing where people sit and tracking the things the office owns. Follow the numbered stages left to right. Chips show who does each step.

Admin

  • Manages floor plans, seats and layout
  • Sets floor Properties — area & cost
  • Approves / rejects seat requests
  • Assigns department colours
  • Runs assets, movements & verification

Employee

  • Views their own seat & the floor map
  • Requests a seat change or swap
  • Books meeting rooms
  • Tracks the status of their requests
  • No admin or approval controls

Switch roles any time from the Admin / Employee toggle in the top bar.

Module A

Seating & Workplace

From an employee’s seat request to the map updating — and everything Admin controls around it.

1Employee

See my seat

Open My Seat to view your current seat, floor and zone — or find it on the floor map.

2Employee

Request change / swap

Ask for a vacant seat, or a swap with a colleague. Add a reason and remarks, then submit.

3System

Admin notified

The request lands in Admin’s Seat Requests inbox and an email-style alert is sent.

4Admin

Approve or reject

Admin reviews the details. Approve and the map updates automatically; reject with a reason.

5Employee

Seat updates

On approval the seat is reassigned (swaps exchange both) and the employee is notified of the outcome.

Admin also runs the floor directly. Allocate, move, block or maintenance any seat · edit the layout (rooms, walls, seats) · set Floor Properties (carpet + super built-up area, rental, maintenance, overhead & misc cost) · colour the map by department · and track Meeting Rooms — availability, bookings, a utilization chart, and a “still using this room?” prompt that frees idle bookings.

Module B

Asset Lifecycle

Every physical asset is QR-tagged and tracked from onboarding through moves, checks and disposal.

1Admin

Register & tag

Each asset is added to the Register with a generated QR code and a printable label.

2Anyone

Scan to open

Scan the QR to open the asset passport — identity, location, custodian, photos and full timeline.

3AI + Admin

Verify condition

Monthly check: AI suggests a condition & confidence; Admin accepts, remarks or flags for repair.

4Admin

Move (governed)

Request → AI condition suggestion → approve → in-transit → receipt scan updates the location.

5Admin

Analyse

Asset Analytics rolls up value, category, condition and verification compliance.

New / Good Fair Damaged / Beyond repair AI-assisted step
AI moments are assistive, not automatic. The system suggests a condition and highlights a changed area — a person always makes the final call.
